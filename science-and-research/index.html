Research approach

From observations to evidence.

Our research framework connects reasoning, device integration and everyday outcomes, with a clear method for examining each stage.

01

Logic & interaction

Does the reasoning hold?

Replay and simulation can test rules, missing readings, state changes and failure handling.

VALIDATION FOCUSExamine rules, state changes and exception handling.
02

Real integration

Does it work with the device?

Test a named device and platform with real permissions, disconnections and operating conditions.

VALIDATION FOCUSEvaluate device coordination in everyday operating conditions.
03

Real-world outcomes

Did everyday life improve?

Define the baseline, action and observation window. Consider other influences and user feedback.

VALIDATION FOCUSCompare outcomes with a baseline and account for other influences.

Our research commitment

Clear methods. Meaningful progress.

Our research commitment is to connect a clear question with traceable observations, a method that can be reviewed and outcomes grounded in everyday life.

As research progresses, we will share the context, methods and findings together, so people can understand how an insight was reached and what it means for their home.
