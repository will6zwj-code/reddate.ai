A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

At home with your questions.

Can I download the App today?

The official release links have not been supplied yet. The download page will show verified platform links when ready. You can explore the App preview now without signing up.

Will I need to replace my devices?

The direction is to work with supported devices. The release compatibility list is not yet confirmed, so please wait for the published requirements before buying additional hardware.

Do I need a hub or sensors?

Requirements depend on the feature and the available observations. A device list, permissions and any hub requirements will be stated with the released App.

Are the scores and insights real household results?

No. The supplied App screens are a prototype with example scores, device states and insights. They do not demonstrate a live scoring engine or measured savings.

What will the App cost?

Release pricing and any subscription terms have not been confirmed. They will be clearly stated before a paid service is offered.

Where can I get setup or installation help?

Home assessment and installation are intended as a separate support route, not a condition for downloading. The verified support channel and service scope will be added when confirmed.
