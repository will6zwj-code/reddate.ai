Noticing the space. Understanding the life.

Our long-term vision for places that become more understandable, responsive and connected to the needs of the people within them.

Long-term vision

MORE THAN CONNECTED DEVICES

A space has a life of its own.

It has a layout, changing light and temperature, familiar routines and different needs. Our ambition is to understand the relationships between these things — and help people make better sense of the places they live.

SpaceEnvironmentDevicesEveryday life

OUR DIRECTION

01

Begin with the home.

Connect available observations to rooms, needs and understandable insights. The home is our starting point.

02

Build spatial understanding.

Develop clearer relationships between places, environmental conditions, devices and everyday choices.

03

Explore wider possibilities.

Investigate other living and care environments, with each setting’s evidence and responsibilities defined separately.
