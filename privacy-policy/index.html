02

03

How and why information is used

We use information to deliver the pages you request, remember your language choice, maintain website reliability and security, respond to enquiries, handle privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not use website information for targeted advertising or to build behavioural profiles of households.

We use the legal grounds available for each activity under applicable law, including your request for a service, necessary operational purposes where permitted, legal obligations and consent where required. Reading this policy or simply browsing is not blanket consent to unrelated processing. Where consent is required, we will explain the specific choice and how to withdraw it.

04

No sale, advertising trade or AI training

Any product feature involving personal information and AI will explain its purposes and applicable choices before that processing begins.

07

Cookies and language preferences

We do not add advertising cookies, social-media pixels or audience-analytics scripts to this website. Your language choice is stored in your browser as “reddate.website.language” and can also appear in the page address as “lang”. The saved preference remains until you change it or clear this site’s browser storage. Blocking storage may prevent us from remembering your choice.

Cloudflare may use necessary security or traffic-management cookies when relevant protections operate. Its network may also receive connection-error reports from supported browsers. These functions support delivery and security. You can manage cookies and website storage in your browser; blocking necessary technologies can affect access. Optional tracking that requires consent will not be enabled without that choice.

08

How long information is kept

We keep correspondence and request records only for as long as needed to respond, carry out agreed follow-up, resolve a complaint or dispute, and meet applicable legal obligations. The period depends on the nature of the enquiry, whether it is resolved and any required retention. When no longer needed, we will delete or irreversibly anonymise information under our control, subject to lawful retention requirements.

Website and security logs held by infrastructure providers follow the retention periods or criteria in their service arrangements and privacy notices. Browser language storage is controlled by you. Where a backup copy cannot be removed immediately, deletion follows the relevant backup cycle; information retained solely for a legal requirement will not be reused for unrelated purposes.

09

Protecting information

Our website is available over HTTPS. We limit the information requested through the website and use hosting and network security services. We will take reasonable technical and organisational steps appropriate to the information we handle, including limiting access to those who need it. No internet transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a personal-information incident, we will assess it, take appropriate containment and remedial steps, and notify affected people and relevant authorities when required by applicable law. You can report a suspected privacy issue using the contact below.

10

Your choices and rights

You may ask us whether we hold information about you, request access or a copy, seek correction, request deletion, object to or restrict a use, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Additional rights, including transfer of information, apply where the relevant law provides them. We will explain any lawful exception or retention requirement that affects a request.

Email our privacy contact with enough detail to identify the information and your request. We may ask for proportionate verification to protect your information, without requesting unnecessary identity documents. We aim to respond within 30 days and will follow any applicable statutory deadline. If more time is permitted and needed, we will explain why and tell you what happens next. Any lawful fee or refusal will be explained before proceeding, with available complaint options.

Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. We will explain if a requested change prevents us from replying or providing a particular service. You can browse without identifying yourself and can use a pseudonym for general enquiries where practical.

11

Children and sensitive information

This website is intended for a general adult audience and does not offer child accounts or seek children’s personal information. Please do not send children’s information, health records, identity documents or other sensitive material unless it is necessary for an agreed request and an appropriate collection process has been explained. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a child has sent.

Where a service requires children’s or sensitive personal information, we will provide the relevant notice and obtain the consent or other authorisation required by applicable law before that collection. This includes guardian consent and special protections for children under 14 where mainland China’s rules apply.

12

Regional rights and complaints

The commitments in this policy apply to our handling of information within its scope. Local mandatory rights remain available, and the applicable law depends on the circumstances. In Australia, relevant rights and complaint avenues arise under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply. In mainland China, relevant rights include information, access, copying, correction, deletion, restriction or refusal, consent withdrawal and explanations under the Personal Information Protection Law.

In Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Act provides relevant access, correction and consent-withdrawal rights. In Hong Kong, the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance provides relevant access and correction rights and protections relating to data use. These laws do not create identical rights or deadlines in every location.

If you have a concern, contact us first so we can investigate and provide an explanation and proposed resolution. You may also approach the relevant privacy authority or use other remedies available under applicable law. In Australia this may be the OAIC; in Singapore, the PDPC; in Hong Kong, the PCPD; and in mainland China, the competent cyberspace administration or other authority responsible for personal-information protection. Our complaint process does not limit those rights.
